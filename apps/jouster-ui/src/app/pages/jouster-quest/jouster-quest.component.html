Controls

  • Arrows / WASD move and choose
  • Z / Enter / Space talk and confirm
  • X / Esc open the menu and go back
  • I items, E equipment (armor, shields, accessories)
  • M world map (explored areas)
  • N mute sound

Click the game screen to give it keyboard focus. Save at the inn or the chapel in Aldmere. A keyboard is required; touch controls are not supported yet.
